How do I place an order?＋

Browse Products, open a product page, choose the available option and quantity, then add it to your bag and continue to checkout.

How do I track an order?＋

Open Track Order from the hamburger menu or footer and enter your order number and customer email.

Will crypto be available at checkout?＋

The checkout is designed for a server-side crypto payment provider. Live payments remain disabled until that integration is configured and verified.

Where do I find documentation?＋

Documentation can be linked from every product page and from the central Documentation page.

How is shipping calculated?＋

The cart currently shows a configurable complimentary-shipping threshold. Final carrier rates, destinations, and timelines must be connected before launch.

Can I change my delivery address?＋

The final address-change window should be defined in the shipping policy. Contact support as soon as possible for an existing order.

What is the return policy?＋

The Returns & Refunds page contains draft structure. Final product eligibility and time windows must be approved before launch.

How do I contact support?＋

Use Contact Us and include the order number if your question is about an existing order.
