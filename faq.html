How do I place an order?＋

Open All Kits, choose a product, select the strength or variant you want, set the quantity, and add it to your cart. Review your cart, continue to checkout, enter your shipping information, choose a shipping method, and select your payment method.

How do I track an order?＋

Go to the Track Order page and use your order number and the email used at checkout. Tracking information is added once the order has been fulfilled and a carrier tracking number is available.

What shipping options are available?＋

Standard tracked shipping is $4.99. Express tracked shipping is $14.99. Orders of $250 or more unlock free standard tracked shipping. When free shipping is unlocked, it replaces the $4.99 standard option; express remains available for $14.99.

How long does shipping take?＋

U.S. Standard delivery is typically about 3–5 business days, while U.S. Express delivery is typically about 1–3 business days. Worldwide delivery is typically about 6–8 business days. Delivery estimates begin after the order is processed and can vary by destination or carrier.

Do you offer same-day shipping?＋

Eligible orders placed before 2 PM PST are scheduled for same-day fulfillment from Florida. Carrier pickup, weekends, holidays, payment verification, and address issues can affect when a package actually enters transit.

What payment methods can I use?＋

Checkout currently offers Crypto and Cash App — Bitcoin. Regular Crypto lets you choose from the supported currencies shown on the payment page. Cash App — Bitcoin creates a Bitcoin quote and lets you send BTC from Cash App to Aspen Labs using the Bitcoin network.

How does Cash App — Bitcoin work?＋

Select Cash App — Bitcoin at checkout. The payment page shows your USD order total, a live BTC quote locked for about 60 minutes, the exact BTC amount, a QR code, and Aspen Labs’ Bitcoin receiving address. Send only BTC on the Bitcoin network, then use the “I’ve sent payment” step so the order can be manually verified.

How are crypto payments confirmed?＋

Your payment page locks the displayed quote for the payment session. After you send the exact amount to the displayed address on the correct network, the payment is manually verified before fulfillment. Keep your order ID and transaction details in case support needs them.

Where can I find COAs or product documentation?＋

Use the documentation link on the product page when one is available. For a specific batch document or COA that is not posted yet, contact support with the product name and, if applicable, your order number.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?＋

Contact support as soon as possible with your order number and the corrected address. Once an order has already been packed, transferred to a carrier, or shipped, an address change may no longer be possible.

What is the return or refund policy?＋

Aspen Labs lists a 30-day money-back guarantee. Contact support with your order number and the reason for the request so eligibility and the next steps can be reviewed for the specific order.

How do I contact support?＋

Use the Contact Us page. Include your order number for payment, shipping, tracking, address, return, or refund questions so the order can be located faster.
